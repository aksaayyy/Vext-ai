# Vext Free Tier Limits

Vext is built on a high-performance, free-tier stack to keep the service free for developers and creators. To ensure fair usage, the following limits apply:

## 👤 For Regular Users
- **Anonymous Users**: 5 extractions per month (tracked by IP/Cookie).
- **Signed-in Users**: 50 extractions per month.
- **Bonus Credits**: Earn **+5 extractions** for every 3 unique card shares.
- **File Exports**: Unlimited ZIP downloads for generated cards.

## 💻 For Developers (v1 API)
- **API Key Rate Limit**: 5 requests per minute.
- **Daily Quota**: 50 extractions per day (shares include your personal quota).
- **Concurrent Extractions**: 1 active extraction at a time.

## ⚡ Infrastructure Status
- **LLM Failover**: Automatically switches between Groq, NVIDIA NIM, and OpenRouter to ensure 99.9% extraction success.
- **Audio Limits**: Supports videos up to 20 minutes in length.

---
*Last updated: April 2026. Limits are subject to change based on provider availability.*
